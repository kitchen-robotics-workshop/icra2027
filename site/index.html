01 / The workshop

The kitchen is a frontier for embodied intelligence.

Understanding a recipe is only the beginning. A kitchen robot must handle food that deforms, breaks, flows, and changes with heat; use tools under contact; and coordinate long tasks in spaces designed for people.

This workshop brings together robotics, food science, materials, design, and healthcare. We connect today’s challenges in robotic cooking with a broader question: what becomes possible when robots, food, and human experience are designed together?

Through talks, posters, demonstrations, and discussion, we aim to develop shared challenge problems, new collaborations, and a research roadmap for the next decade.

01 / MORNINGROBOT + FOOD
NOW

Robot manipulation for kitchen tasks

What does it take to make robotic cooking reliable in the real world? Explore the hardware, sensing, learning, and control behind physical food manipulation.

  • Food perception
  • Multimodal sensing
  • Food-state monitoring
  • Planning & control
  • Tool-based manipulation
  • Robot learning
  • Foundation models / VLA
  • Soft hardware
  • Modelling & simulation
  • Sim-to-real transfer
  • Human–robot interaction
  • Safety & deployment
02 / AFTERNOONROBOT + FOOD + HUMAN
FUTURE

Re-designing food, bodies, and senses

What if food and eating systems were part of the design space? Explore new materials, sensory capabilities, and ways for robots to interact with human needs.

  • Programmable food
  • Edible robots
  • Robot-friendly food design
  • Robotic oral processing
  • Chewing & feeding simulators
  • Taste sensing
  • Smell sensing
  • Sustainable materials
  • Personalized interaction
  • Culinary experiences
